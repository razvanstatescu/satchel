**Method.**
- **Frames.** I extracted every frame from f230 to f2320 at 960 px, selecting by index (`select=between(n,…)`).
  - A note for other verifiers: this mp4's first frame has pts 0.042 s, so `-ss N/30` lands one frame early.
    Select by index instead.
- **Scans.** Four automated scans: consecutive-frame differences, an 8×8-block A-B-A flicker scan, a blank-frame scan,
  and a caption-band scan. The caption-band scan measures caption ink in the top 300 px of every frame (white ink in
  C1), so it catches empty or dimmed caption frames.
- **Inspection.** I read frame-by-frame strips around every row, caption swap, cut and owner change, and used
  full-resolution crops for every measurement. Where a comparison was needed, I extracted the v4 frames.
- **Sources.** I read them only to understand intent: `H1/timing.ts`, `P4/index.tsx` (`Teammate`), `transitions.ts`
  and `Main.tsx`.

the owners' decision), 0 regressed.** The three r3 rows in this range are fixed as well. Across 8.0–76.5 s:

- The caption band is empty on only two frames: the first frame of a new scene (f1725) and the arrival frame of the
