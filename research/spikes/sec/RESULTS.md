| 1 | EVM: keccak256(unsigned EIP-1559 serialization) is exactly the hash to sign; fields parse back out; non-tx payloads are refused | `node evm_preimage.mjs` → `out_evm.txt` | **28/28 PASS.** The preimage `0x02f283aa36a7…80c0` is 52 B for an ETH transfer and 115 B for an ERC-20 transfer. A raw signature over keccak(preimage), assembled by viem, is byte-identical to `account.signTransaction`, and `recoverTransactionAddress` returns the sender. A strict RLP decoder matches `parseTransaction` on chainId, nonce, to and value. Refused: legacy (EIP-155 and pre-155), type-1, type-4 (7702), the 0x05 authorization preimage with chain_id 0, EIP-191, EIP-712 (0x1901), non-canonical RLP, trailing byte, chainId 1, contract creation, `approve` calldata. The pre-155 signing hash does not depend on the chain. |
| 2 | BTC: sha256d(BIP143 preimage) == bitcoinjs `hashForWitnessV0`; outpoint, amount and sighash come from a fixed layout; non-ALL types are refused | `node btc_preimage.mjs` → `out_btc.txt` | **27/27 PASS.** The P2WPKH preimage is always **182 B**. The outpoint txid is at [68..100] in internal order (reverse it for explorers), vout at [100..104], amount at [130..138], sighash at [178..182]. Raw-signing sha256d(preimage) gives the same txid and the same bytes as the PSBT path. NONE, SINGLE, ANYONECANPAY variants and 0x41 are refused, as are altered scriptCodes and wrong lengths. The legacy SIGHASH_SINGLE "hash=1" constant is identical for unrelated txs, which is why raw-hash signing is dangerous. `preimageFromPsbt(psbt, i)` rebuilds the preimage from public PSBT fields, and its sha256d equals the hash a chainsig.js-style mock signer captures. |
| 3 | SOL: the MPC signs the message bytes themselves; recentBlockhash extraction; durable-nonce detection same as agave; legacy + v0 + ALT; instruction allowlist | `node sol_message.mjs` → `out_sol.txt` | **20/20 PASS.** An Ed25519 signature over the raw message is the tx signature (`verifySignatures`). A durable nonce is detected when ix[0] is the system program and data starts with `04000000`. In that case the "recentBlockhash" field is the nonce value. With an ALT, the destination is not in the message bytes. Refused: Assign, CreateAccount, SPL Approve, the `\xffsolana offchain` message (parses as unsupported version 127), trailing byte, multi-signer. |
| 4 | Do Rust strict parsers agree with JS and how big are they in wasm? | `node gen_vectors.mjs && cd rust-parsers && cargo test --release` (+ `--features alloy`); wasm sizes via `cargo build --release --target wasm32-unknown-unknown --features …` → `out_wasm_sizes.txt` | **19/19 cross-language vectors agree** (hand-rolled and alloy-rlp variants). Sizes (opt-level z, LTO): panic/fmt baseline 16,494 B. On top of it: BTC +0.6 KB, EVM +3.4 KB (alloy-rlp +4.5 KB), SOL +3.6 KB, all three +7.9 KB (alloy +9.1 KB). |
