MetaBUILD will take you from idea to market ready in 8 weeks.
Write, test and deploy scalable decentralized applications on the most developer-friendly blockchain using the languages you already know – even Javascript.
NEAR is here to help reimagine your world and empower users to have full control and responsibility over their data. Build simple, scalable and secure dApps, DeFi, Web3, Gaming, Digital Art/Collectibles, infrastructure apps and tools, or migrate an existing EVM app to NEAR.
Join the NEAR MetaBUILD Hackathon and start coding.
Stay tuned for online meet-ups, educational workshops and mentor days!
Requirements
What to Build
Build an app using NEAR in one of the below tracks.
Native – For brand new projects. Only projects built completely during the submission period will be considered.
Web2 to Web3 – For Web2 projects making the switch to Web3. Only for projects that are on the market already, but during this hackathon, added blockchain functionality.
What to Submit
Open source code repository. Provide a URL to your open source code repository for judging and testing. Your code must have an open source license like one of the below:
MIT - https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT
Apache 2.0 - https://opensource.org/licenses/Apache-2.0
3-Clause BSD - https://opensource.org/licenses/BSD-3-Clause
Demo Video. Include a video (under 3 minutes) that demonstrates your submission. Videos must be uploaded to YouTube, Vimeo, or Facebook Video and made public.
Select Prize Tracks. Identify which track your project fits into and if you used and sponsor tools.
