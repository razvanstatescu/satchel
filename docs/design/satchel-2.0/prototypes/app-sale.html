How Satchel checks this

Every signature this Satchel has issued is recorded on-chain. Your browser just checked each one against Bitcoin testnet4, Ethereum Sepolia and Solana devnet: none can still move the coins you are buying.

What it doesn't cover

  • Chains, tokens and accounts other than the three derived addresses on the three listed networks.
  • Any signature made after the purchase.
  • The honesty of NEAR's signer network.
  • Testnet4 reorgs: one confirmation can be undone by anyone mining two difficulty-1 blocks.
  • Coins another wallet later sends to an outpoint an old signature already spends (marked encumbered).

The contract doesn't run this check; this app does, by reading each chain. Buy stays disabled until it passes.
