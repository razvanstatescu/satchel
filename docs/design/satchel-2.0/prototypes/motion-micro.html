Press, roll, sheets, toasts, signing, charms, coins, the clean-history check. Every replay button replays one scene.

Press and roll

The primary button is a sticker: it presses 4 px down into its own shadow and comes back. While a request runs it stays latched down and its label rolls to the busy verb; it pops back up when the request settles. Secondary buttons only change fill.

Toasts

One at a time, above the pinned button, never on it. A toast rises 16 px and settles (260 ms), stays 4 s (paused on hover and focus), and leaves faster than it came (160 ms). A new toast waits for the old one to leave.

Signing progress

Honest milestones, never a percentage. Each finished step gets its check stuck on and the line fills down to the next one. The only things that move while you wait are the spinner on the current step and the elapsed time. The waiting line changes at 20, 60 and 150 seconds by a quiet roll.

      Charms

      Clean-history check

      The buyer's browser checks each past signature; results are revealed in list order, at least 220 ms apart, as they arrive (never held back longer). A finished row gets its mark stuck on and its word rolls in. Clean: the verdict sticks on and Buy rises out of the page. Needs attention: the row gets a danger bar, the verdict arrives without celebration, Buy stays flat, and pressing it nudges once and points at the reason.
